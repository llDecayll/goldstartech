Who we are

We are Goldstar Tech — a full-stack growth partner for businesses that are done with guesswork.

Founded in Bangalore, we combine the craft of a design studio, the rigour of an engineering team and the accountability of a performance agency. Our clients span healthcare, real estate, agri-tech, solar energy, hospitality and retail across India, the UK, UAE, USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

Everything we ship is built to be measured. If a page, post or campaign cannot be tied to a business outcome, we ask why it exists.

How we work

A process built for momentum.

Four stages, repeated. Each cycle compounds what the last one learned.

01

Discover

We audit your market, competitors, analytics and current channels. Every engagement starts with evidence, not assumptions.

02

Design

Strategy, creative direction and technical architecture are drafted together so the plan is buildable and measurable from day one.

03

Build

Websites, campaigns, content and software ship in tight sprints with weekly checkpoints — you always know what is live and why.

04

Compound

We measure, interpret and reinvest in what works. Reports explain the numbers; the numbers fund the next experiment.
